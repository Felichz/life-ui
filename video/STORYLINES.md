# LifeUI product film: three storylines (second film, November 2026)

The first film (a day in forty seconds, below) showed the day loop. The app is now
changed by talking to a coach in the chat, so the second film is about that. Shared
assumptions as before: silent, 1920x1200, 30 to 45 s, light and dark, every screen
the real app; the conversation is a scripted run of the real agent.

## A. From the chat to the calendar (chosen)

One conversation carries the film. The person writes that the elbow hurts, the coach
looks it up and lays out a plan with sources, the person says yes, the roadmap card
waits on "Do it", Goals shows each session's exercises with the guide links, and at
9:00 the first session runs and is closed through the chat for its tempos. The moment
to remember: a sentence becomes 50 sessions on the calendar.

## B. A day, now with a coach

The first film shortened (start, run one thing, close 9/10, Review) with the coach in
between: asked to add something, asked what is left, proposing the week's plan.

## C. Not a chatbot

Giant type against the app: "It does not just answer" (the confirm card), "It puts it
on your calendar" (the sessions), "And it checks itself" (a plan sent back). Bolder,
shows less of normal use.

# First film (November 2026): three storylines


Shared assumptions: silent (the portfolio plays clips muted), 1920x1200 (16:10, the
portfolio's clip frame), 30 to 45 s, rendered in light and dark. Every screen is the
real app: its own DOM and CSS captured from the running build, with the demo data
the README screenshots use. On-screen copy in English.

## A. One honest moment (recommended: one real task carries the whole film)

Device: a number lands. Form: one continuous take inside the app.

The viewer follows one activity, "Write report", from the day's plan to its close.
The camera never leaves the app: it pushes into the running card, the Finish click
opens the closing ritual, the score is picked, and the tempos it earns fly into the
day's meter before the camera pulls back to the Review.

- First two seconds: the focus card filling the frame, the clock ticking 23:07,
  "Running since 3:00 PM", and the title "One thing at a time".
- Beats: hook (one activity running) -> plan (the day in time blocks, later ones
  locked) -> close (Finish, pick 9 out of 10, the preview shows +78) -> payoff
  (+78 lands, the tempo bar passes the daily reference) -> claim ("Tempos only go
  up") -> end card (mark, name, lifeui.medanolabs.com).
- Look: the app's own light or dark canvas, Inter, indigo only on what is clicked,
  amber only on tempos.

## B. A day in forty seconds

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one frame that rebuilds.

The Today screen holds still while the day runs through it: the day starts at 8:12,
activities start and close one by one, the log fills, blocks pass from "Now" to
"Passed", and the evening ends on the Review.

- First two seconds: the empty Start-the-day screen with "Good morning" and the
  cursor on "Start the day".
- Beats: start the day -> morning closes land in the log -> afternoon block turns
  "Now" -> a close at 9/10 -> end day -> Review -> end card.
- Look: same app canvas; a large clock in the margin carries the time-lapse.

## C. Not a todo app (the bold one)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: hard cuts between giant type and the UI.

Three giant lines, each cut against the screen that disproves it: "Not a todo app"
(the time-block plan), "Not a habit tracker" (no streaks, the tempo bar), "Not a
time tracker" (the closing ritual asks how it went). Then "A HUD for real life".

- First two seconds: "Not a todo app" filling the frame in black Inter on the app's
  canvas.
- Beats: three claims, each followed by its screen -> the closing ritual as the
  answer -> end card.
- Look: type at 14% of the frame height, the app canvas, one indigo word per line.
